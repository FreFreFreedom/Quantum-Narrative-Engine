## The Room Before Use

This is the thinking room. It has no agenda, no deadlines, no cards on the table. It exists so the project can think about itself — not what it does, but what it is, and what it might become.

The subject is fractal consciousness systems. The app reads a film, a character, and a country as the same object at different scales. That is the deep structure of everything in here. What that means, where it goes, what it demands — that is what the room is for.

Nothing has to be settled by the time someone leaves. The point is the thinking itself, carried forward in a form that makes sense later, to someone who was not here.
